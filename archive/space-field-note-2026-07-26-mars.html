Mars

The rusty archive of a wetter world

Perseverance's Three Forks Sample Depot Selfie
NASA's Perseverance Mars rover took a selfie with several of the 10 sample tubes it deposited at a sample depot it is creating within an area of Jezero Crater nicknamed "Three Forks." The image was taken by the WATSON (Wide Angle Topographic Sensor for Operations and eNgineering) camera on the end of the rover's robotic arm on Jan. 22, 2023, the 684th Martian day, or sol, of the mission. What is it made of?

Basaltic rock rich in iron-bearing minerals; iron oxides give Mars its red color. Its thin atmosphere is mostly carbon dioxide, with dust that can spread planet-wide.

Why it’s fascinating

The hook

Mars is a crime scene for planetary scientists: dry river valleys, lake beds, clays, and minerals all hint that liquid water once shaped the planet. The question is whether chemistry ever crossed into biology.

A tiny thought experiment

Stand above Mars with a transparent science visor. The colors are not just scenery — they are chemistry: gases, minerals, sunlight, and temperature writing a planetary fingerprint in front of you.
