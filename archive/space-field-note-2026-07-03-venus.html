Three Galaxies in Pavo
Some 190 million light-years away, far beyond the bright stars and nebulae of the Milky Way, these three galaxies are drawn together by gravity in a mesmerizing cosmic dance. Clearly distorted by galactic-scale gravitational interactions, large spiral galaxies NGC6769 and NGC6770 are seen face-on, with luminous galactic disks scarred by obscuring interstellar dust lanes. Their young blue star clusters along drawn out spiral arms are spawned in star forming regions that result from collisions of massive molecular clouds. What is it made of?

A rocky silicate planet with an iron core, wrapped in a crushing carbon-dioxide atmosphere and sulfuric-acid cloud layers.

Diameter12,104 km
Surface pressure~92 Earth atmospheres
Surface temperature~465°C
Atmosphere~96.5% CO₂
Why it’s fascinating

The hook

Venus is not the closest planet to the Sun, but it is the hottest planet because its thick CO₂ atmosphere traps heat in a runaway greenhouse. Its surface pressure feels like being nearly a kilometer underwater on Earth.
Imagine this

A tiny thought experiment

Stand above Venus with a transparent science visor. The colors are not just scenery — they are chemistry: gases, minerals, sunlight, and temperature writing a planetary fingerprint in front of you.
