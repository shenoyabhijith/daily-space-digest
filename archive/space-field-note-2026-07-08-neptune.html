Why it’s fascinating

The hook

Neptune receives very little sunlight, yet it has some of the fastest winds in the Solar System. Something inside this cold blue planet is still powering wild weather.
Imagine this

A tiny thought experiment

Stand above Neptune with a transparent science visor. The colors are not just scenery — they are chemistry: gases, minerals, sunlight, and temperature writing a planetary fingerprint in front of you.
