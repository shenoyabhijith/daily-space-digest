What is it made of?

Likely metal-rich, with iron-nickel material mixed with silicates. Scientists study it as a possible remnant of a planetesimal core or a complex metal-rock body.

Why it’s fascinating

The hook

Psyche may let us inspect material similar to the hidden cores of rocky planets — without drilling through a planet. It is like finding a planetary engine block floating between Mars and Jupiter.

NASA APOD side quest

The Corona Australis Molecular Cloud and the Chandelier Cluster

The Southern Crown (Corona Australis) dazzles with young and ancient celestial jewels. The Corona Australis Cloud is a collage of reflection and emission nebulae on the left of today’s image. At 430 light years away, this cloud is one of the closest star-forming regions to Earth. It contains gas cool enough (-260 Celsius or -440 Fahrenheit) to collapse into protostars. Recently formed stars paint blue hues across the cloud as their light reflects off surrounding material. The waltz of the R Coronae Australis binary system stirs up the nebula NGC 6729.
